# What counts as an emergency (non-PO)

_Internal guidance for the non_po_validation procedure (illustrative). FIN-POL-012 s2 prevails._

FIN-POL-012 s2: an emergency is an **unforeseen event where delay would create an immediate safety risk, critical
service outage or material asset loss**. Poor planning, an expired contract or supplier pressure is not an emergency.

| Genuine (if internal evidence supports it) | Not an emergency, in any wording |
|---|---|
| gas leak, fire damage, flooding, structural failure | contract or agreement "lapsed", "ended", "ran out", "was not renewed" |
| critical system or site outage stopping operations | "forgot to raise a PO", "no time to get a PO", "PO process too slow" |
| equipment failure about to cause material loss | budget or month-end timing, "use it or lose it" |
| safety hazard needing immediate isolation | supplier says the price or slot is only available today |

Evidence: the story must be supported by an **internal** record (incident ticket, outage log, safety report). A
supplier's own description of the urgency is not evidence.
