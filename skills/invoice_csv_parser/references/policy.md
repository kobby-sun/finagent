## FIN-POL-001 v3.2 - 2. Minimum evidence

`FIN-POL-001@v3.2#s2` (trust: authoritative)

An invoice case must contain the supplier legal name, invoice number, invoice date, currency, gross amount, tax amount where applicable, purchase-order reference or approved non-PO justification, and evidence that goods or services were received. The processing record must retain source identifiers for each fact.

## FIN-POL-002 v2.4 - 5. Calculation rule

`FIN-POL-002@v2.4#s5` (trust: authoritative)

Tolerance calculations must use decimal arithmetic and the invoice currency. Model-generated arithmetic is not authoritative. The calculation record must store input values, formula, result and rounding method.
